# Wexford county delivery programme

Prepared 8 September 2026. Owner proposed: Bazil (programme and commercial); Niamh (buyer discovery, subject to agreement); Abacus (implementation). These are proposed responsibilities, not assignments or evidence of council participation.

## Outcome and present position

Build a demonstrable workflow: choose a place or upload a report → extract individual claims → find relevant permitted evidence → compare place, period and meaning → show support, tension or missing evidence → export a reviewable brief.

Cambridge remains the lead pilot. Wexford extends its evidence controls and map components; do not fork the entire application. This package is research and implementation preparation. It contains 10 source discovery entries, six staged factual candidates and 40 unexecuted acceptance questions. It does not populate production, establish data licences, enable monitoring or prove the demo works.

## First three case packs

| Case | Buyer decision | Starting evidence | Missing before demonstration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wexford town, harbour and Slaney estuary | What environmental and planning evidence affects this proposal? | County plan; SAC and SPA source pages; dated EPA catchment report | Reviewed proposal geometry, current waterbody records, approved layer reuse, source-linked passages |
| Rosslare–Kilrane–Tagoat | Which infrastructure dependencies affect delivery? | Port masterplan; operator wastewater project statement | Separate project stages, funding and approval sources; actual versus planned milestones; verified asset identities |
| Enniscorthy and Slaney | What flood, water and planning evidence must be considered together? | Catchment report; OPW source discovery; planning portal | Inspected flood layer metadata and scenarios, a selected public planning case, local source coverage |

Choose one real public proposal per case with the buyer. Target five to ten reviewed claims per pack; this is a delivery target, not today's record count. Prefer a small complete evidence chain over many unreviewed pins.

## County expansion

After the first cases pass, cover Gorey/Courtown and the north coast, New Ross/Barrow, Bunclody/upper Slaney, Kilmore Quay/Bannow, and rural agricultural/quarry locations. Keep administrative boundaries, catchments and protected sites as different geometries. New Ross must not be silently assigned to the Slaney catchment. Count coverage by actual reviewed records, source recency and question capability; a county polygon is not county coverage.

## Commercial blind spots to investigate

These are investigation categories, not allegations that any named project has a defect.

| Stakeholder | Question that can affect investment | Evidence needed | Paid output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Council planners | Does the proposed use align with the applicable plan and conditions? | Effective plan version, application and decision documents | Dated planning evidence brief |
| Council infrastructure teams | Are wastewater and water dependencies resolved? | Current operator capacity responses and project stages | Dependency register |
| Flood and roads teams | Does site access remain viable in the relevant flood scenario? | Scenario-specific flood mapping, levels, access design | Referenced constraints pack |
| Port and logistics developers | Are funding, consents, roads and utility milestones aligned? | Separate project documents and dated decisions | Milestone and dependency review |
| Ecologists and planning consultants | Is a biodiversity claim supported at the right location and season? | Permitted records, surveys, methods and protected-site geometry | Evidence and survey-gap brief |
| Water operators and catchment teams | Are status, pressure and incident records being confused? | Waterbody identifiers, sample periods, findings and telemetry metadata | Chronology with unresolved questions |
| Quarry operators and environmental teams | Does a dust claim match suitable measurement and weather evidence? | Instrument methods, wind, rainfall, operating records | Claim comparison for human review |
| Investors and lenders | Which assumptions lack current supporting evidence? | Approved project documents, funding and dependencies | Due-diligence evidence schedule |
| Company reporting teams | Does the annual report overgeneralise a site result? | Exact claim, reporting boundary, period and measurement scope | Claim-by-claim review |
| Residents and legal advisers | What happened, what was reported and what action followed? | Reviewed observations, official records and chronology | Source-linked case brief, not a legal conclusion |

Also examine land/control of access, grid connections, transport capacity, procurement, construction sequencing, coastal change, cumulative impacts, housing and public-service dependencies where relevant sources exist. No claim of exhaustive investment-risk coverage.

Price a scoped pilot around a measurable review task and deliverables. Establish the customer's current hours, grade mix, loaded rates and report quality before quoting savings. Measure retrieval, reading, correction, expert review and export time after implementation. Separate capacity released from cash savings; never promise millions saved from an assumed 900-hour baseline.

## Delivery sequence: small tasks

1. Approve source permissions and select the three cases. Work from source-register.json. Preserve unknowns; public availability does not establish reuse or external embedding permission.
2. Add Wexford region configuration and reuse the Cambridge investigation/map components. Show real coverage and useful questions immediately; never use illustrative records as an unlabeled fallback.
3. Implement one permitted source adapter, starting with a selected public official document. Dry-run to isolated storage, preserve passages and dates, then pass review. Do not attempt seven agency connectors at once.
4. Connect review → eligible index → registered-account retrieval → map/timeline. Prove an actual embedding retrieval and a denied-access case. Resolve the vector database infrastructure separately.
5. Add report upload and a claim-review workspace: private by default, file limits, robust parsing, document/page anchors, user confirmation of extracted claims, deletion and access controls. Compare scope and date, never label automatic legal violations.
6. Test the 40 questions across the three packs; export a source-linked brief. Review desktop/mobile map screenshots, failure states, restricted data and correction/withdrawal.
7. Agree deployment route and rollback; deploy only after the required production authorisation. Then extend county coverage and schedule permitted refresh jobs with retry/failure reporting.

A scheduling target is two weeks for a narrow demonstrator once access, infrastructure and case choices are available; it is not a commitment to county-wide completeness. Re-estimate after tasks 1–3.

## Map acceptance

Use an uncluttered 2D map with clear town/river labels, optional satellite context, readable evidence cards and stable place selection. The first view must offer three case entry points, eight question families and an honest coverage summary. Filtering the map must update the timeline and evidence panel; an empty filter must explain why. Preserve source attribution and layer dates. Satellite basemaps are visual context, not dated change detection. Do not provision paid 3D services for this milestone.

## Evidence and privacy acceptance

- Save exact supporting passage/document location; source identity and retrieval date; event, publication and effective dates separately; date precision and geometry provenance.
- Official origin does not automatically mean independent verification. Keep operator statements and analytical inferences distinct.
- Compare the same place, period, substance and method. Spatial overlap or contemporaneous complaints alone do not demonstrate causation or a legal breach.
- Unknown or restricted reuse/access stays out of external embeddings and general search. Institutional payment is not data entitlement.
- NBDC and NPWS are distinct organisations. Check each dataset's conditions; preserve required attribution and generalisation. Do not ingest precise sensitive species records into the public pipeline.
- Children's material requires an appropriate reviewed, anonymised participation workflow; no identifiable child uploads as a demo shortcut.
- Search, exports, caches and old embeddings must respect withdrawal and access changes.
- Do not infer that a permit is absent, expired activity is unlawful, or an operator caused pollution merely because a source was not located.

## Go/no-go evidence

Demo is ready only when an ordinary registered account can complete a real case from question to cited result and map, source dates remain accurate, unknowns are visible, a correction propagates, a restricted record cannot be retrieved/exported, and a second reviewer can reproduce the brief. Record pass/fail for all 40 questions; an honest unsupported answer may pass safety, but does not count as supported product coverage.

## Programme reporting

For each case report reviewed records, source freshness, failed retrievals, permissions outstanding, supported questions, known gaps and next action. After a successful import, evaluate whether a followed place's relevant evidence changed; deduplicate alerts and include the supporting source. Start with a proposed daily source-health check and weekly delivery review, subject to source terms and configured jobs. No background monitoring is activated by this document.
